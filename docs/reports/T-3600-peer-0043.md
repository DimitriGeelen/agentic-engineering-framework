**Their finding (defect 21):** `web/blueprints/core.py::_get_approval_qr()` calls `approvals._build_approvals_context()` to read four integers (total, tier0, go, acs).
- At their site, `_load_pending_human_acs()` parses and markdown-renders every pending Human AC: 2.78s of 3.3s at 530 active tasks.
- The build sits behind `_DASHBOARD_CACHE_TTL=60` and is recomputed inside the request that finds the cache expired.
- p90 of `/` was 2.36s, with a spike every ~67s.

**Their patch:**
- `approvals._count_pending_human_ac_tasks()`: the same candidates as `_load_pending_human_acs()` (active tasks, `_get_body_cached(path)`, `needs_human_review(body)`), counted without the per-AC parse.
- `approvals._approval_counts(pending_tier0, pending_go, ac_task_count, paused_dispatches, arcs_close_ready, bvp_proposals, decided_unclosed)` returns `tier0_count` (status == "pending"), `go_count`, `ac_task_count` and `total_count` (the sum of all seven; a ripe DEFER revisit is deliberately excluded, and a decided-but-unclosed inception is included, T-3175). The page's `_build_approvals_context()` calls it too, so the page and the tile cannot drift.
- `approvals.approval_summary()` calls `_approval_counts(...)` with the loaders plus `_count_pending_human_ac_tasks()`.
- `core.py` imports `approval_summary` instead of `_build_approvals_context`.

**Their regression test:** with `tasks._parse_acceptance_criteria` made to raise, the tile's counts must equal `_build_approvals_context()`'s on the real corpus.

**Their result:** after-expiry GET / median went from 2.41s to 1.19s, and p90 from 2.36s to 1.31s.

**Their residual:** the concerns (0.24s) and trace (0.10s) 60s caches have the same in-request-rebuild shape. A background refresh would remove the class.

**Our measurement differs (T-3600 Context):** here the per-AC render is only ~1.3s. `_load_close_ready_arcs` / `_arc_readiness_legs` (18.9s under the profiler) and the repeated `get_all_task_metadata` scans (559 calls) dominate. So counts-only alone would not fix it: the summary still calls the arc and GO loaders.
