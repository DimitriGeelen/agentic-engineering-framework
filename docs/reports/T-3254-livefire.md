# T-3254 — live-fire evidence (AC5) and its negative control (AC6)

Generated by `tools/t3254-livefire.sh`. Re-run it rather than trusting this copy;
it is a snapshot, and the whole point of the arc is that recorded results and live
results must be separable.

## What this proves

The loop's unfixed failure mode is `exit no-signal`: the agent stops before the
budget gauge trips, backlog remaining, and nothing restarts it. M2 cannot reach
that case — it triggers on budget-critical. Here a session that has stopped early
is driven to completion by the cron path alone, and the budget gauge is never
consulted.

## What it does NOT prove — stated, not implied

The session is a real TermLink PTY session receiving real injections from the real
driver, resolving its target through project config the way the installed cron line
does. Transport, bounds, busy check, ledger and terminal signal are all exercised
end to end. What it is **not** is a Claude session reasoning about a directive: the
"work" is a shell script that consumes one backlog unit per turn. Read this as
covering everything up to the moment the prompt lands in the session, and nothing
after it.

## The negative control is the load-bearing half

Without it, "the driver continued the work" and "the work finished on its own"
produce identical evidence. That indistinguishability is what made the E9 ceiling
result meaningless, and it is the single most repeated failure in this arc. The
identical run with `enabled: false` leaves the backlog at 3 of 3.
