Frequent friction that pushes agents toward bypasses erodes the gate more than any single defect.
2. **OBS-452 (finding 20) — handover emits a complete-looking handover with no content.** Not in 832's top five. I would rank it first. A `None`-filled handover looks finished, `/resume` does not warn, and session state disappears without any signal. That is the silent-failure case the directives rank highest.
3. **OBS-413 (finding 13) — handover pushes without a flag.** Agreed it matters, but it is a ruling before it is a fix: CLAUDE.md states the push is intended. Keep it in the top five as the decision to make.

5. **OBS-392 + OBS-409 (findings 11 and 12) — BVP ranks stubs.** Not in 832's top five. Stub tasks land mid-scale or in identical vectors, so selection by quadrant can pick work nobody has scoped. This is ranking integrity, and it affects every BVP-driven choice.
