## The sentence the page cannot get wrong

**The object is the Beibringungspflicht, never the Nachweis.**

The Nachweispflicht — that a fact must be established before a decision — is
legitimate and is never touched. What Streichliste identifies is the
**Beibringungspflicht**: the duty laid on the citizen to fetch the paper.

Copy that puts *Nachweis*, *Kontrolle* or *Prüfung* in the object position is
wrong in the most damaging available direction — it reads as wanting to abolish
checks, which is the objection the project exists to survive. Test every sentence
you write against it.

## The logic chain, and why the page is two steps

| | Step | State |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A **Nachweispflicht** exists | Legitimate. Never touched. |
| 2 | It produces a **Beibringungspflicht** | **What we identify** |
| 3 | Narrowed to where **the state already owns the data** | Established: 627, 3 registers |
| 4 | Whether the state **can and may** retrieve it itself | **The ambition. Open — 0 of 627.** |

A single band reading `627 · 0 · 3` lets a reader average those into one
impression. Split into *what this run establishes* and *what should follow from
it*, each number sits against the claim it supports, and the zero stops reading
as failure.

**Do not resolve the tension between the headline and the zero by weakening the
headline.** The headline states what the instrument is for; the two-step block
directly beneath is what stops it claiming the list of droppable duties already
exists.

## Two registers of language

The landing page speaks the way a person speaks — *Zettelwirtschaft*, *Bote
zwischen Behörden*, *Papier*, *Botengang*. Die Liste, Die Belege and Die Struktur
speak the way the record speaks — *Beibringungspflicht*, *Abrufbefugnis*,
*Sprosse 5*. Neither is a simplification of the other: the everyday words say
what it **costs**, the exact words say what is **claimed**. Never mix them inside
one sentence. They meet in exactly one place — the Führungszeugnis example.
