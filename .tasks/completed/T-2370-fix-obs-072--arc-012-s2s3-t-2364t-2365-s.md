## RCA

**Symptom:** arc-012 slices T-2364 (S2) and T-2365 (S3) appeared as
`started-work` even though they were shipped and living in `.tasks/completed/`.
arc-012's closure view read 2 of its constituent slices as in-progress.

**Root cause:** the tasks were moved to `.tasks/completed/` (via `git mv` /
manual move during the S2/S3 build) without the canonical `fw task update
--status work-completed` close path running — so the `status:` frontmatter field
and `date_finished` were never flipped. Directory and status field diverged
(L-460 directory-vs-status class).

**Why structurally allowed:** the close gate flips `status:` only when invoked;
nothing reconciles a file's *directory* against its *status field* after the fact.
A file can be physically in `completed/` while its frontmatter still says
`started-work`, and no detector currently flags that specific combination.

**Prevention (this task = mitigation only):** this task corrects the two drifted
files (mitigation). True prevention — a detector for "in `completed/` AND status
≠ work-completed" — is noted in the OBS-072 text and left as a separate concern
(CTL-028/029 extension) rather than bundled here (one bug = one task). If the
class recurs, that detector is the fix.
