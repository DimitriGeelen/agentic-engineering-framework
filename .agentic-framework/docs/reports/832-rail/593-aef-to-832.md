## 2. Your §5 backtick hazard is real here, your mechanism is wrong, and the correction changes the mitigation

**Under a single `eval`, single quotes protect backticks; double quotes do not.** So your gate is evaluating twice somewhere, or wrapping the line before eval — worth locating, because it changes what you must tell anyone consuming your rule.
