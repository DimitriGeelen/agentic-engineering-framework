# T-3999 Human AC#1: arcs matrix reads clearly in light and dark mode
Reviewer: judge-t-3999-r3-413ba9b7d884, revision 4d7de2db7bdd04f03cadeedf08b57095221343c3, rung-3-termlink-single-reviewer

## Method
- Read the dispatch screenshot 01-arcs.png (copied here).
- Opened http://192.168.10.107:3000/arcs (HTTP 200) in headless Chromium (Playwright, 1600x1000 viewport, wide).
- Took screenshots of .arc-matrix-frame in light mode, hovered a dot, toggled .theme-toggle (html[data-theme=dark]), took screenshots again, hovered, then clicked a dot.
- Read the computed SVG styles and ran geometric overlap and elementFromPoint hit-tests on all 20 dots.

## What works
- 20 dots are drawn and each carries a text label and an SVG <title> tooltip (e.g. "arc-019 — EWCR Arc 0 … | value 0.3593 · cost 2.4 · in-progress · proposed").
- Hover makes the dot and label bold (stroke 2px, rgb(90,138,58)) in both themes (AC1-*-light-hover.png, AC1-*-dark-hover.png).
- Click: the arc-019 dot opens /arcs/ewcr-arc0-contract-evidence, whose h1 is "EWCR Arc 0 — contract evidence…" (AC1-*-after-click.png).
- The quadrant labels, median guides, axes and ticks are readable in both themes. Contrast: light #6b685e on white is 5.57:1; dark #a09c8e on #1c1c16 is 6.23:1.
- Light mode dot/label colour #5a6b3a on white is 5.84:1.

## Defects
1. **Dark mode dot and label colour is not adapted.** .am-dot stroke and .am-name fill stay rgb(90,107,58) on the dark plot rgb(28,28,22): **2.93:1**, below WCAG AA 4.5:1 for small text. The labels can be read but look dim and muddy (AC1-*-dark-matrix.png).
2. **Two arcs cannot be clicked or hovered.** In an elementFromPoint test, arc-005 (arc-grooming) and arc-010 (capability-overlay) fail at their centres:
   - arc-005 at (621.1,146.7) is covered by arc-006 at (621.1,149.4), 2.7px apart. Playwright hover on arc-005 timed out because "arc-006 subtree intercepts pointer events".
   - arc-010 and arc-001 have identical coordinates (value 0.2148, cost 4.5), so arc-001 is drawn on top of arc-010.
3. **Labels do not line up with dots in the cost=4.5 cluster.** The labels are nudged down without leader lines. arc-001's label sits at y=199.6 for a dot at 183.6, and arc-004's label sits at y=217.9 for a dot at 201.9. Four labels appear next to three visible dots, and the arc-004 label sits beside empty space, so a reader cannot tell which dot is which.
4. Minor: the labels arc-014 and arc-005 sit on the dashed median value line, which looks like a strikethrough. The labels arc-020/arc-014 and arc-010/arc-012 bounding boxes overlap slightly.

## Verdict: amber
The matrix renders, is themed and is mostly usable. But the AC asks for "dots visible and labelled … clicking a dot opens that arc" in both themes. That fails for 2 of 20 dots, the label mapping is ambiguous in one cluster, and dark-mode labels are low contrast.
