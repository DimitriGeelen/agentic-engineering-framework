## What I ran (my own headless Chromium, 1440x900, not the producer's numbers)

Followed the criterion's steps: open /fabric, then real clicks on the site-menu links BVP -> Terminal ->
Fabric graph -> Tasks, counting requests for the target URL and timing click -> drawn. Three full walks.

| step | requests for the page | kind | click -> drawn (3 walks) | drew |

- Direct fetch of /bvp by curl: 2.64 s. So the menu load of BVP (2.7-2.9 s) is about one server render;
  the two-fetch behaviour the T-5130 reviewer measured (5.2-5.7 s) is gone.
- No console or page errors on any of the four pages in walks 2 and 3. (Walk 1 logged one
  "Cannot read properties of null (reading 'innerText')" on /terminal; that was my own probe reading
  document.body during the navigation, and it did not recur once the probe waited for the body.)
- Tasks, timed separately with the xhr and the content change observed (8 clicks, from /fabric and
  from /fabric/graph): URL pushed 0.71-1.32 s, content swapped 0.87-1.59 s, same document every time,
  one xhr. The producer recorded 0.9 s. The ordinary menu-link markup
  (`hx-target="#content" hx-swap="innerHTML" hx-push-url="true"`) is byte-identical to the parent of
  56f4e68ff, so this is "as before".

## Things I saw that do not match a clean picture (reported, not hidden)

1. One Tasks click, in my first walk, took about 20 s before my probe saw /tasks in place. I could not
   reproduce it in 8 further clicks (all about 1 s). I did not find its cause. It fits the defect T-5147
   describes (first task-board load after a review-ledger write recomputes readiness inline, 9-22 s;
   T-5147 is in progress and other reviewers were writing ledger rows during my run), but that is my
   inference, not something I measured. It is on the unchanged swap path, not on this task's change.
2. "Instantly" is about 1 s for Tasks on this host (curl of /tasks: 0.35 s; load average 9-15 during the run).
3. After an ordinary menu swap the page is scrolled 132 px down, so the menu bar is off the top of the
   viewport (see live-tasks screenshot; same after a swap to /fabric). Markup unchanged by this task, so
   pre-existing; the document title also stays that of the page the user came from.
4. The criterion says "Fabric Graph"; the menu entry is labelled "Explorer" (Architecture menu). The URL
   in the criterion is right, so the step can be followed.
5. Rendered page /settings/models (05) is an unstyled fragment (a list of model names, no shell). It is
   not a menu page and is not touched by this task.
6. The working tree has an uncommitted change to web/shared.py (T-5147 readiness refresh). It does not
   touch NAV_FULL_LOAD_ENDPOINTS or the menu.
