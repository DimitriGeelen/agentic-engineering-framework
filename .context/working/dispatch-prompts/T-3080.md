## The distinction you must not collapse

1. **Grant TTL** — how long an approval, once given, admits the command. This is what
   T-3080 unifies.

ago as expired and the operator could no longer act on their own queue. Stale *pending*
requests are T-3079's problem, filed separately. If you touch `approvals.py` at all, it is
only to read it.
